Van recovery in Peterborough and on the A1(M)

Most van calls come from tradespeople and delivery drivers whose working day stops when the van does. We recover vans up to 3.5 tonnes: panel vans, Lutons, tippers, pick-ups and minibuses, from the A1(M), the A47, the parkways and industrial estates across Peterborough. Tell us the make, wheelbase and roof height, and how much load is on board.

Loaded vans

A fully loaded van may be over its weight limit for the truck, or the load may need to be secured first. Where we can, we take the van with its load. If it’s too heavy, we’ll tell you before we set off. Tools and stock stay locked in the van, and we deliver it to your yard, your garage or a commercial dealer.

Can you recover a van with the load still on it?

Usually yes, within the bed’s weight limit. Tell us roughly what is on board so we can check before we send a truck.
