A1(M) & A1 breakdown recovery

The A1(M) runs from Alconbury past Peterborough to junction 17, then becomes the A1 north through Wansford and Stamford. It is our busiest road.

The stretch we cover

  • J14 Alconbury: where the A1(M) starts, near the A14 junction.
  • J15 Sawtry: B1043 to Sawtry and Stilton.
  • J16 Norman Cross: A15 to Yaxley and Peterborough.
  • J17 Peterborough: Peterborough Services, the A1139 Fletton Parkway and the A605.
  • A1 north: Wansford (A47 junction), Stamford, and on towards Colsterworth.

Tell us the junction you last passed and your direction (northbound or southbound). That is all the driver needs to find you.

If you break down on this road

  1. Get off the road if the car still moves: hard shoulder, lay-by, slip road or verge.
  2. Hazards on, everyone out on the side away from traffic, and behind the barrier if there is one.
  3. Can’t get out of a live lane? Keep your seat belt on and call 999.
  4. Call us with the road, the direction and the nearest junction, marker post or lay-by.

Where we take the car

Off the A1 and to your home, your garage or our operator’s yard, or on to anywhere in the UK if you are a long way from home. For long runs, see car transport.

Peterborough Services and Wansford

Peterborough Services at junction 17 is the safest place to stop on this stretch if the car is still moving. Tell us where in the car park you are and which fuel station or restaurant you’re nearest. North of junction 17 the road becomes the A1, and at Wansford it crosses the A47. The slip roads there are short and busy; if you stop on one, get everyone well clear.

Long way from home?

Lots of A1 breakdowns are drivers heading between London, the Midlands and the North. If the car can’t be fixed locally, we can take it, and usually you and your passengers, all the way home. You hear the price for the whole trip before we set off.

FAQ

Straight answers

How much is recovery from the A1(M) near Peterborough?

It’s priced on the distance from the A1(M) to where the vehicle needs to go, and you get a fixed figure before we dispatch.

Can you take me and my car home if I live far away?

Yes, we can transport the car and in most cases carry passengers in the truck cab, seat belts permitting. Tell us how many people when you call.
