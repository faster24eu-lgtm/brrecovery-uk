A141 & A142 breakdown recovery

Two long fen roads that meet at Chatteris. Straight, fast and lined with drains, with very few safe places to stop.

A141

A141

From Huntingdon past Warboys to Chatteris, then north past Wimblington and around March to the A47 at Guyhirn. It is the main route from the A14 to Wisbech and King’s Lynn for a lot of traffic.

A142

From Chatteris south-east past Mepal and Sutton to Ely, then on to Soham and Newmarket. Open fen for most of the way to Ely.

If you break down on this road

  1. Get off the road if the car still moves: lay-by, verge, slip road or the next village.
  2. Hazards on, everyone out on the side away from traffic, and well away from the car.
  3. Stuck in a live lane or in a ditch with water? Call 999 first.
  4. Call us with the road, your direction and the last village or junction you passed, or share a live location pin.

Out on the fen

These roads are hard to describe: one stretch looks like the next. A live location pin or a what3words address is the quickest way to get a truck to you. See how to share your location.

Nearby: Chatteris, March, Ely, Warboys and Huntingdon.

Common problems on fen roads

  • Cars in drains. In fog, ice or heavy rain, cars leave the road and end up in a verge or a drain. If there’s water, get out and call 999 first.
  • Punctures from farm debris on the edge of the road.
  • Breakdowns at night, a long way from any lights. Stay well away from the car, with a torch or your phone light on.

We’ll ask you whether the car is level, on its side or partly in water, so the driver brings the right winch and kit.

FAQ

Straight answers

Do you cover the A142 at night?

Yes, 24/7. Send your location and we’ll give you an honest arrival time.

Can you pull a car out of a fen drain?

Yes, with a winch, once everyone is safe. If the car is in water or anyone is hurt, call 999 first. Then send us a photo so the driver brings the right kit.

Do you cover the A141 March bypass?

Yes, and the roads into March. Tell us which roundabout you last passed.
