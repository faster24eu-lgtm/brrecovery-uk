A15 & A16 breakdown recovery

Two roads head north out of Peterborough into Lincolnshire. Both are long, fast single carriageways with few places to stop.

A15/A16
  1. Home
  2. Roads
  3. A15 & A16

A15

From Peterborough past Glinton and Northborough, around Market Deeping, then on to Bourne and Sleaford. Busy at rush hour with commuters into the city.

A16

From the A47 near Eye, past Crowland and around Spalding, then on to Boston. This stretch used to be the A1073 and was renumbered as the A16 in 2011, so older sat-navs and maps may still call it that.

If you break down on this road

  1. Get off the road if the car still moves: hard shoulder, lay-by, slip road or verge.
  2. Hazards on, everyone out on the side away from traffic, and behind the barrier if there is one.
  3. Can’t get out of a live lane? Keep your seat belt on and call 999.
  4. Call us with the road, the direction and the nearest junction, marker post or lay-by.

Where the roads meet the towns

The A15 bypasses Market Deeping, then runs straight through open farmland to Bourne and on to Sleaford. The A16 bypasses Crowland and Spalding, then continues past Sutterton, where it meets the A17, to Boston. Both roads have long, fast stretches between villages, with farm entrances and slow tractors. Overtaking crashes and breakdowns at night are common.

What to tell us

The road, your direction and the last village or roundabout you passed. On the A16 it helps to know whether you’re north or south of Crowland or Spalding. See Market Deeping & Bourne, Crowland, Spalding, Boston and Sleaford.

Farm traffic and mud

At harvest time, tractors and trailers use both roads all day, and mud on the road makes them slippery in the wet. Slow down near farm entrances, and if your car has slid off into a verge, don’t try to drive it out. Get everyone safe and call us for a winch.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is the old A1073 the same road as the A16?

Yes. The Peterborough to Spalding road via Crowland became part of the A16 in 2011.

Do you cover the A15 between Bourne and Sleaford?

Yes. It is a long road through open country, so a live location pin helps the driver find you quickly.

Do you cover the A16 north of Spalding to Boston?

Yes, including the Sutterton roundabout where the A16 meets the A17.
