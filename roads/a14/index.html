A14 breakdown recovery

The A14 carries a huge amount of freight between the Midlands and Felixstowe. When a car breaks down among the lorries, it needs moving quickly.

The stretch we cover

  • Thrapston: the A605 junction for Oundle and Peterborough.
  • Brampton: where the A14 meets the A1.
  • The Huntingdon southern bypass, the newer section running south of the town.
  • Swavesey to Girton towards Cambridge, with St Ives just to the north.

If you break down on this road

  1. Get off the road if the car still moves: hard shoulder, lay-by, slip road or verge.
  2. Hazards on, everyone out on the side away from traffic, and behind the barrier if there is one.
  3. Can’t get out of a live lane? Keep your seat belt on and call 999.
  4. Call us with the road, the direction and the nearest junction, marker post or lay-by.

The Huntingdon southern bypass

The A14 was rerouted south of Huntingdon in 2019 onto a new dual carriageway with three lanes in places and wide hard shoulders. The old road through the town became the A1307. If your sat-nav is out of date, it may not show the new road, so check the road signs and tell us exactly what you can see.

Thrapston and Kettering

West of the A1, the A14 runs past Thrapston, where it meets the A605, and on to Kettering and the M1. This stretch is busy with lorries. If you break down here, tell us the junction number and direction.

What to tell us

Eastbound or westbound, the last junction, and any marker post number. See broken down on the A14, Huntingdon, St Ives, Thrapston and Kettering.

Brampton and the A1

At Brampton the A14 meets the A1, with a complicated junction of slip roads and link roads. If you break down here, tell us whether you’re on the A14 or the A1, and which way you were heading. A location pin is the quickest way.

FAQ

Straight answers

Can you recover a van from the A14?

Yes, vans up to 3.5 tonnes. Tell us the wheelbase and how much load is on board.

Does the new A14 near Huntingdon have a hard shoulder?

The newer southern bypass section does. Use it if you can, then get everyone out on the left and behind the barrier.

Is there 24 hour recovery on the A14?

Yes, day and night, around Huntingdon, St Ives, Brampton and Thrapston. You hear the price before we set off.
