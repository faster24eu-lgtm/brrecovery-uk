Peterborough parkway breakdown recovery

Peterborough’s parkways are fast dual carriageways with short slip roads and almost no hard shoulder. A breakdown on one needs moving fast.

The parkways

  • Fletton Parkway (A1139): from the A1(M) at junction 17 across the south of the city.
  • Frank Perkins Parkway (A1139): the eastern side, past Stanground and Fengate.
  • Nene Parkway (A1260): the west side, through Orton and Longthorpe.
  • Soke Parkway (A47): across the north of the city.
  • Paston Parkway (A15): north towards Werrington and Glinton.

Tell us the parkway, which way you were heading and the last junction or roundabout you passed.

If you break down on this road

  1. Get off the road if the car still moves: hard shoulder, lay-by, slip road or verge.
  2. Hazards on, everyone out on the side away from traffic, and behind the barrier if there is one.
  3. Can’t get out of a live lane? Keep your seat belt on and call 999.
  4. Call us with the road, the direction and the nearest junction, marker post or lay-by.

Rush hour on the parkways

At peak times the parkways slow to a crawl around the busiest junctions, near the Queensgate and city centre exits, Hampton and the A1(M), and Bretton and the hospital. Older cars overheat in the queues, and clutches give up. If the car stops in slow traffic, put the hazards on, get out only if it’s safe, and call 999 if you are stuck in a live lane.

Getting off the parkway

If the car is losing power, take the next exit rather than stopping on the main carriageway. The roundabouts at the bottom of the slip roads usually have a verge or a layby nearby where a truck can load safely. See car recovery in Peterborough.

FAQ

Straight answers

There is no hard shoulder. What do I do?

If you can, get onto a slip road, a verge or the next junction. If the car stops in a lane, put the hazards on and, only if it is safe, get out and behind the barrier, then call 999 before calling us.

Which parkway am I on?

If you are not sure, share a live location pin and we’ll work it out. Fletton and Frank Perkins are in the south and east, Nene in the west, Soke in the north and Paston to the north-east.

Can you take my car from a parkway to a Peterborough garage?

Yes. Most parkway breakdowns end at a garage or dealer in the city. Tell us which one.
