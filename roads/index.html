Why road guides?

The quickest way to get a truck to you is to tell us exactly where you are. On the A1(M) that means the junction and direction; on a fen road it might mean the last village and a live location pin. Each guide lists the junctions, landmarks and lay-bys that help the driver find you, plus what to do while you wait. See also how to share your location.

The roads we’re called to most

The A1(M) past Peterborough, the A47 between Wansford and Wisbech, and the Peterborough parkways are our busiest roads. Out on the fen, the A141, A142 and A605 see a lot of breakdowns at night and in fog. In South Lincolnshire, the A15, A16 and A17 carry heavy lorry traffic all year.

FAQ

Straight answers

Which roads do you cover?

The A1 and A1(M), A47, A14, A15, A16, A17, A605, A141, A142, A428, A10, A43 and the Peterborough parkways, plus every road in between.

How do I tell you where I am on a main road?

The road, your direction and the last junction or village you passed. A live location pin is even better.
