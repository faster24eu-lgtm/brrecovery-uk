A47 breakdown recovery

The A47 crosses our whole patch from Rutland to the Fens. Busy and dual through Peterborough, narrow and exposed east of it.

The stretch we cover

  • Uppingham to Wansford: hills and bends through Rutland, single carriageway.
  • Wansford: junction with the A1.
  • Soke Parkway: the dual carriageway across the north of Peterborough.
  • Eye to Thorney: single carriageway, heavy with lorries.
  • Guyhirn: the A141 junction for March.
  • Wisbech bypass, and on towards King’s Lynn.

If you break down on this road

  1. Get off the road if the car still moves: hard shoulder, lay-by, slip road or verge.
  2. Hazards on, everyone out on the side away from traffic, and behind the barrier if there is one.
  3. Can’t get out of a live lane? Keep your seat belt on and call 999.
  4. Call us with the road, the direction and the nearest junction, marker post or lay-by.

Fen stretch

East of Thorney there are few lay-bys and steep verges into drains. If you cannot get fully off the road, stay out of the car and well away from it, and call 999 if it is in a live lane.

Uppingham and the Rutland hills

West of Wansford the A47 climbs through Rutland past Morcott and Uppingham, with steep hills, sharp bends and junctions hidden behind crests. Heavy vehicles and cars towing caravans struggle on the climbs, and clutches and gearboxes give up. If you stop on a hill, get well off the road and tell us which side of Uppingham you’re on.

East to King’s Lynn

Beyond Wisbech the A47 runs on through West Walton and Walpole to King’s Lynn and the junction with the A17 and A10. We cover the whole road from Uppingham to King’s Lynn, day and night. See broken down on the A47, Thorney, Wisbech and King’s Lynn.

FAQ

Straight answers

Do you cover the A47 at night?

Yes, 24/7, including the Thorney to Wisbech stretch.

Is the A47 between Peterborough and Wisbech dangerous?

It is a busy single carriageway with heavy lorries, few lay-bys and drains beside the road east of Thorney. If you break down there, get well away from the car, and call 999 if it is in a live lane.

Can you take my car from the A47 to King’s Lynn?

Yes, or anywhere else. Tell us where it needs to go, and you hear the price before we set off.
