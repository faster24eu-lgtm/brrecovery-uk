Long distances, quiet nights

South Lincolnshire is mostly farmland, with long straight roads between towns and very little lighting at night. A breakdown on the A15, A16 or A17 after dark can leave you a long way from help. Before you call, note the last village you passed and your direction, or share a live location pin. If you’re on a narrow road with a drain alongside, stay out of the car and well away from it while you wait. See how to share your location.
