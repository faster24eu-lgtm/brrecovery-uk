When a jump start works

If the car clicks, the dash flickers or the engine turns over slowly, it is usually the battery. A booster pack will often start it in seconds. Then drive for at least 30 minutes or get the battery tested.

When it will not

If there is no click at all, a smell of fuel or burning, or the starter spins without the engine catching, it is likely something else. The driver can load the car and take it to your garage on the same visit.

Stop-start and hybrids

Modern cars with stop-start, AGM batteries or hybrid systems have specific jump points. Our drivers use the points in the handbook and a booster with surge protection.

Jump start service across Peterborough

A flat battery is the most common reason a car won’t start, especially on cold mornings and after a car has stood for a week or more. We send an operator with a professional jump pack to your home, workplace or car park, across Peterborough, the Deepings, Stamford, Yaxley, Thorney and the villages around them.

Why batteries go flat

  • The car hasn’t been driven for a while.
  • Lights or an accessory were left on.
  • Lots of short trips that never recharge the battery.
  • An old battery: most last around four to six years.
  • A charging fault, if the battery goes flat again after a drive.

If the car starts after a jump but the battery warning light stays on, don’t drive far. See car won’t start.
