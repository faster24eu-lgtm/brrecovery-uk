Classic car transport from Peterborough

Old cars are fragile, often irreplaceable and sometimes don’t roll. We move them gently, whether it’s a concours car or a project from a barn.

How classics are moved

  • Wheel straps over the tyres, never through suspension or round bumpers.
  • Low loading angle, so low cars don’t ground.
  • Winch and skates for cars that don’t run or have seized brakes.
  • Drip tray on the bed for cars that leak oil.

Shows and events

Burghley, Stamford, Peterborough and the classic shows around the region are busy every summer. Book early, tell us the date and when the car needs to be there, and we’ll bring it back afterwards.

Restorations and barn finds

A car that has sat for years may have flat tyres, seized wheels and no brakes. That’s fine. Send us a photo and tell us whether the wheels turn and whether it can be steered. We plan the kit around it.

For long runs, see car transport. For auctions, see auction collection.

Covered and open transport

Most classics travel on an open flatbed, strapped by the wheels, which suits short and medium runs. If your car is very valuable or the journey is long, ask about covered transport, and we’ll tell you honestly what we can arrange. Either way, the car is loaded slowly and checked before it leaves.

FAQ

Straight answers

Can you move a classic that doesn’t run?

Yes. Tell us whether the wheels turn and steer, and send a photo. We use a winch and skates where needed.

Can you take my classic car to a show at Burghley or Stamford?

Yes. Book early in the show season, tell us the date and when the car needs to be there, and we bring it back afterwards.

Do you carry classic cars that leak oil?

Yes. We put a drip tray under the car on the bed.
