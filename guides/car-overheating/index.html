Car overheating?

What to do

  1. Pull over safely as soon as you can.
  2. Switch off the engine.
  3. Never open a hot radiator or expansion cap. The coolant is under pressure and can scald you badly.
  4. Wait at least 30 minutes before opening the bonnet to check.

Common causes

  • Low coolant from a leak: a split hose, radiator or water pump.
  • A cooling fan that isn’t working.
  • A stuck thermostat.
  • A head gasket failure, often with white smoke and milky oil.

When to call us

If there’s steam, a big leak, milky oil, or the temperature goes straight back up after cooling, don’t drive it. We take it to your garage or home. In summer, cars often overheat in queues on the A1, the A47 and at the Cross Keys bridge at Sutton Bridge. See breakdown recovery.
