FAQ

Straight answers

What should I do first if my car breaks down?

Get off the road if you can, put the hazards on, and get everyone out on the side away from traffic. Call 999 if the car is stuck in a live lane. Then call us.

Are these guides legal advice?

No. They are practical help from people who recover cars every day. For legal or insurance questions, speak to your insurer or a solicitor.
