Common non-runner jobs

  • An MOT failure going from the test centre to a garage for repairs.
  • A project car bought online, collected from a barn or a back garden.
  • A SORN car moved to a new address.
  • A car going to an authorised treatment facility (scrap yard), or to a buyer.

Things that make it harder

Flat tyres, seized brakes, a locked steering column, a car boxed in by others, or a narrow gravel drive. None of these stop us, but tell us on the call so the driver comes with the right kit.

Scrapping it?

Only an authorised treatment facility can scrap a car and issue a Certificate of Destruction, and you must tell the DVLA. We can deliver it to the ATF you choose. Read moving a non-runner for the checklist.

Non-runner car transport around Peterborough

A non-runner might have a dead engine, seized brakes, flat tyres, no keys or a locked steering wheel. None of that stops it being moved. Our operators use a winch to pull a car onto the bed, skates under wheels that won’t turn, and a tow dolly where needed. Send us a photo and tell us what you know, and we’ll price it properly.

Common pick-up places

Driveways, garages, back gardens and farm buildings, auction sites, police pounds, and garages that can’t fix the car. If the car is in a tight space, such as a garage with a narrow door or a back lane, tell us so the right truck comes. See how to move a non-runner.

FAQ

Straight answers

Can you move a car with flat tyres?

Yes, with skates under the wheels and the winch. Tell us how many tyres are flat when you call.

Do you buy cars for scrap?

No, we only move them. We can take your car to an authorised treatment facility or to a buyer you have found.
