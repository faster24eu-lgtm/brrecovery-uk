How it works

Fenline Recovery is an independent call-connection service. When you call, we take the details, agree the price with you, and send the nearest suitable recovery operator in our network. That operator does the recovery and is responsible for the vehicle while it is on their truck.

Who comes out

Local, insured recovery operators with flatbeds, winches and skates, who work the A1, the A47 and the Fens every day. We only use operators who agree to our fixed-price rule: the price you are told is the price you pay.

Where we work

Our patch is Peterborough and the country around it: the Fens from Thorney and Whittlesey to Wisbech, March and Chatteris; South Lincolnshire from Stamford and the Deepings to Spalding, Holbeach and Boston; Huntingdon, St Ives and St Neots; Rutland; and North Northamptonshire from Oundle to Corby and Kettering. Further out, Cambridge, Ely, King’s Lynn, Grantham and Sleaford take a little longer to reach. See every area we cover.

How we price

We don’t publish a minimum price, because every job is different. The distance, the vehicle, whether it rolls and how easy it is to reach all change the cost. Instead, you get one fixed figure on the phone, agreed before anyone sets off, and that is what you pay. The same rule applies at 3am on a bank holiday as at midday on a Tuesday.

What we move

Cars, vans up to 3.5 tonnes, 4x4s, electric and hybrid cars, motorbikes and scooters, campervans, classic cars, non-runners and auction cars. Every vehicle travels on a flatbed. See all services.
