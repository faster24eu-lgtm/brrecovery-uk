Motorbike recovery in Peterborough

Broken down, dropped or crashed? Motorbikes need different kit from cars. We send a truck with a wheel chock and soft straps, so your bike arrives without a mark.

Why bikes need the right truck

A bike strapped badly on a car transporter can fall, crack a fairing or bend a lever before it leaves the lay-by. Our operators load bikes with a front-wheel chock, soft loops round the lower yokes or frame, and gentle compression on the forks. Never over the bars, never across the plastics.

What we recover

  • Road bikes, sports bikes, tourers and adventure bikes.
  • Scooters and mopeds, including delivery scooters.
  • Bikes that have been dropped or crashed and won’t roll properly.
  • Track bikes and off-road bikes with no plates.

Where we cover

Peterborough, the A1(M), the A47 and the fen roads, Stamford and Rutland’s biking roads, Huntingdon, Spalding and Wisbech. Tell us where the bike is and where it needs to go.

Crashed?

Get yourself safe and checked first. Photograph the bike and the road before it’s moved, and keep our invoice for the claim. See accident recovery.

FAQ

Straight answers

Can you recover a motorbike in Peterborough?

Yes, 24/7. Tell us the make and model, whether it rolls, and where it needs to go.

Will my bike be strapped properly?

Yes. Front wheel in a chock, soft loops on the frame or yokes, never over the handlebars or fairings.

Is there motorbike recovery near me in Peterborough?

Yes, day and night across Peterborough, the Fens, Stamford, Huntingdon and South Lincolnshire. Call with the bike’s location and we send a truck with a wheel chock and soft straps.

Can you recover a scooter or moped?

Yes. Scooters and mopeds go on the truck the same way as bigger bikes, strapped without touching the bodywork.
