The A17 runs past Long Sutton and over the Nene at Sutton Bridge on the Cross Keys swing bridge, then on to the A47 near King’s Lynn. When the bridge swings open for shipping, traffic queues, and older cars overheat in the queue. South of the towns, the A1101 heads across the fen to Wisbech.

24 hour car recovery in Long Sutton

  • Breakdowns on the A1101 to Wisbech and the roads to Tydd St Mary and Tydd Gote.

FAQ

The Cross Keys bridge is open and my car has overheated. What should I do?

Switch off, put the hazards on and let it cool. Don’t open a hot radiator cap. If it won’t restart, or if it is blocking traffic, call us with which side of the river you are on.
