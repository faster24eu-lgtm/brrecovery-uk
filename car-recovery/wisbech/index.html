The A47 and the Freedom Bridge

The A47 skirts Wisbech to the south, with busy roundabouts at the junctions for the town and the A1101. In the centre, traffic crosses the Nene at the Freedom Bridge. Breakdowns on the bridge approaches and in the town-centre one-way system are common at rush hour. Tell us the road and which side of the river you’re on, and we’ll pick the right way in.
