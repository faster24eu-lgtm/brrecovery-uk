King’s Lynn is the main town in West Norfolk, at the mouth of the Great Ouse. The A47 skirts the south of the town on its way from Wisbech to Norwich, the A17 joins from Lincolnshire, the A10 comes in from Downham Market and Ely, and the A149 runs north to Hunstanton and the coast. The big roundabouts where these roads meet are busy at rush hour and in summer.

24 hour car recovery in King’s Lynn

  • Cars stopped on the A17 from Sutton Bridge, a road with heavy lorry traffic.
  • Summer breakdowns on the A149 to Hunstanton, with holiday traffic and caravans.
  • Cars that need taking back to Peterborough or further for repair.
