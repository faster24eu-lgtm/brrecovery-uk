Breakdowns in Stamford town centre

Stamford’s stone streets were laid out long before cars. St Mary’s Hill, Broad Street and the roads around the Meadows are narrow, often lined with parked cars, and a recovery truck can’t always stop right beside a broken-down car. If you’re stuck in the centre, tell us the street and the nearest shop or pub, and whether the car can be rolled a few metres to a wider spot. The station car park and the larger supermarket car parks are easy places to load.

Burghley and event days

When the Burghley Horse Trials run in late summer, and on big event days at Burghley House, traffic on the A1 junctions and the B1081 backs up for miles. Cars overheat and clutches give up in the queues. If that happens to you, get off the road where you can and send us a pin; we’ll find the quickest way in.
