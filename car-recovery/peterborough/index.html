FAQ

Straight answers

How much is car recovery in Peterborough?

Local recovery in Peterborough starts from £125. Things that change it: the distance to the drop-off, a non-rolling car, or a low multi-storey that needs a smaller truck. You get the full price on the call.

Can you get a car out of a multi-storey car park?

Yes. Tell us which car park and floor so we send a truck that fits under the barrier height, or the driver uses skates to move the car to a level where it can be loaded.

I have broken down on the A1(M) near Peterborough. What should I do?

Pull onto the hard shoulder or into a lay-by, put the hazards on, and get everyone out on the verge side and behind the barrier. Then call us with the junction number and direction. If you cannot get off the live lane, call 999.
