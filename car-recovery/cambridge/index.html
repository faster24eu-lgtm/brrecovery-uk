Car recovery Cambridge

Cambridge is busy, congested and ringed by fast roads. Whether you are stuck on the A14 or in a city car park, we’ll get the car moved.

Cambridge is surrounded by major roads: the A14 to the north, the M11 to the west, the A428 coming in from St Neots and the A10 from Ely. In the city itself, narrow streets, bus gates and busy car parks make a recovery truck’s job harder, so a precise location helps a lot.

24 hour car recovery in Cambridge

  • Breakdowns on the A14 between Huntingdon and the Girton interchange, and on the A14 towards Newmarket.
  • Cars stopped on the M11 and at the Girton junction with the A428.
  • Cars that won’t start in multi-storey car parks, at the park and rides and at the hospital campus.
  • Planned transport to and from dealers and garages in the city.

For multi-storeys, tell us the car park and floor, so we send a truck that fits under the height barrier or plan to move the car to a level where it can be loaded. See stuck in a multi-storey car park.

FAQ

Do you cover Cambridge?

Can you collect a car from a Cambridge multi-storey?

Usually, yes. Tell us the car park, floor and height barrier, so the right truck comes.
