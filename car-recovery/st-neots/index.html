The Black Cat roundabout

South of St Neots, the Black Cat roundabout is where the A1 meets the A421 to Bedford and the A428 towards Cambridge. It is one of the busiest junctions in the region and has been under major roadworks as National Highways rebuilds it. Lanes, slip roads and speed limits change, so tell us exactly where you are: before or after the roundabout, and which road you came from.
