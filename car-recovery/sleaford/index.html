Sleaford sits at the crossing of two of Lincolnshire’s main roads. The A15 runs south to Bourne and Peterborough and north to Lincoln. The A17 runs west to Newark and the A1, and east to Swineshead and the coast. They meet just north of the town at the Holdingham roundabout. The A153 heads south-east towards Billinghay and Horncastle.
