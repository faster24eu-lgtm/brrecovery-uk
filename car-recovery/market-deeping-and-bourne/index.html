The A15 and A1175 commute

Every weekday the A15 carries commuters from Bourne and the Deepings into Peterborough, and the A1175 links Market Deeping with Stamford and the A1. Most of our calls here are early-morning non-starters on driveways and evening breakdowns on these two roads. Both are single carriageway for long stretches, with farm entrances and few lay-bys. If you stop, pull as far onto the verge as you safely can and tell us the nearest village: Northborough, Langtoft, Baston, Thurlby or Bourne.

Bourne Woods and the A151

West of Bourne, the A151 climbs towards Corby Glen and the A1 at Colsterworth, past Bourne Woods. It’s a quiet road at night. East of Bourne, the A151 runs flat across the fen to Spalding. We cover the whole road in both directions, and take cars to garages in Bourne, the Deepings, Stamford or Peterborough.
