The A15 runs north out of Peterborough past Glinton and Northborough, bypasses Market Deeping and continues to Bourne and Sleaford. The A1175 crosses it at the Deepings, linking Stamford to Spalding. Most of our calls here are breakdowns on these two roads, or cars that will not start at home.

Areas we cover

Market Deeping, Deeping St James, Deeping Gate, West Deeping, Langtoft, Baston, Bourne, Thurlby, Dyke, Morton, Rippingale and the villages along the A6121 towards Stamford.

Roads we cover around Market Deeping & Bourne

  • A15 Peterborough to Market Deeping and Bourne
  • A1175 Stamford to Market Deeping and Spalding
  • A151 Bourne to Spalding
  • A6121 Bourne to Stamford

Where we take your vehicle

Home, your own garage, a main dealer, a body shop or secure storage. If it is late and your garage is shut, we can keep the car overnight and deliver first thing.

Also covering
