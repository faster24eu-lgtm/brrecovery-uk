Car recovery Sawtry & Stilton

Sawtry and Stilton sit right beside the A1(M) south of Peterborough. Many of our calls here start with a car on the motorway.

Junction 15 of the A1(M) serves Sawtry, and the old Great North Road, now the B1043, runs through Stilton parallel to the motorway. To the east lies the Great Fen, with long straight roads past Holme Fen and Woodwalton Fen. Traffic is light, but the roads are narrow and unlit.

24 hour car recovery in Sawtry

  • Breakdowns on the A1(M) between junctions 14 and 16, northbound and southbound.
  • Cars on the B1043 through Stilton and Sawtry, often after coming off the motorway with a warning light.
  • Breakdowns on the fen roads to Holme, Conington and Woodwalton.
  • Cars that won’t start in Sawtry village and at the business parks by the junction.

If a warning light comes on on the A1(M), coming off at junction 15 into Sawtry is far safer than stopping on the hard shoulder. Pull into a safe spot in the village and call us. See the A1(M) junction guide.

FAQ

Straight answers

I have broken down on the A1(M) near Sawtry. How do I tell you where I am?

Give us your direction (northbound or southbound), the last junction you passed and any marker post number, or share a live location pin.

What is the nearest services to Sawtry on the A1(M)?

Peterborough Services at junction 17 is the nearest proper service area north of Sawtry. If your car is struggling, it is often safer to come off at junction 15 into Sawtry than to try to reach it.

Can you take my car from Sawtry to Huntingdon?

Yes. Huntingdon is a short run down the A1. Tell us which garage, and check it is open; out of hours, we can keep the car overnight and deliver it in the morning.
