Narrow streets and the A605 bypass

Oundle’s market place and the streets around Oundle School are narrow and busy at school drop-off and pick-up times. If your car stops in the centre, tell us the street, and whether it can be pushed to a wider spot or a car park. Most through traffic uses the A605 bypass, which runs around the east of the town towards Peterborough and Thrapston.

The Nene valley villages

The villages around Oundle, from Fotheringhay and Nassington to Polebrook, Barnwell and Titchmarsh, sit on quiet lanes with stone bridges, tight bends and few places to pull in. A truck can reach almost all of them, but it helps to know about low bridges, gated drives or a narrow lane. We take cars from the villages to garages in Oundle, Peterborough or Thrapston.
