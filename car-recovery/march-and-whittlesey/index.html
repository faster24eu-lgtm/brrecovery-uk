Whittlesey Washes and the B1040

North of Whittlesey, the B1040 to Thorney crosses the Whittlesey Washes, the flood plain of the Nene. In wet winters the washes fill and the road closes. Never drive into floodwater there: if the car stalls, get out if it’s safe and call 999, then call us. A car that has taken in water should not be restarted, as that can wreck the engine.

March and the A141

March sits on the A141 between Chatteris and Guyhirn, with the bypass to the west of the town and level crossings on the railway through the centre. Most calls in March are cars that won’t start at home, at the station or at the retail parks, and breakdowns on the A141 towards Wimblington and Chatteris.
