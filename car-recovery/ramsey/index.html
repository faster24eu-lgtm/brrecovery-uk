Ramsey grew up around its abbey on an island in the fen. Today the roads in are long and narrow: the B1040 north to Pondersbridge and Whittlesey, the B1096 south to Warboys and the A141, and the lanes east to Ramsey Forty Foot and Ramsey St Mary’s. The Forty Foot Drain runs right beside the road for miles.

What we see around Ramsey

  • Cars that have slid off a fen road into a verge or a drain, especially at night and in frost.
  • Breakdowns on the B1040 and the road along the Forty Foot, where there is nowhere to pull in.
  • Cars that won’t start in Ramsey, Warboys and Bury.
  • Breakdowns on the A141 between Warboys and Chatteris.

Fen roads are hard to describe. A WhatsApp location pin or a what3words address saves a lot of time. If the car is in water, get out and call 999 first.
