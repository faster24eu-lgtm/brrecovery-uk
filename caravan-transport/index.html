Cars, vans, 4x4s and EVs: what we tow

Touring caravans and car trailers that are road legal to tow, using a truck fitted with a tow ball. Twin-axle tourers are fine; we check the weight and length on the call.

What we do not move

Static caravans and park homes need specialist haulage and a site crane or tractor, which our network does not do. We can tell you who to ask.

Before we tow

  • The tyres hold air and are not perished. Old caravan tyres often fail on the first long run.
  • Lights, breakaway cable and jockey wheel work.
  • Inside is packed down and windows and roof lights are shut.

When you need caravan or trailer transport

  • Your tow car has broken down with the caravan attached.
  • The caravan needs taking to a dealer or workshop for a service or repair.
  • You’ve bought a caravan or trailer and don’t have a suitable tow car.
  • A trailer has a broken axle, wheel bearing or hitch.

Tell us the length, weight and type of hitch, whether the brakes and lights work, and where it needs to go. We’ll tell you straight away whether we can take it.

Broken down while towing

If your tow car breaks down on the A1, the A47 or the A17 to the coast, we can arrange to move the car and the caravan. See also motorhome and campervan recovery.
