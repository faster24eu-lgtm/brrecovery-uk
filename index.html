What we do

Built for the roads around Peterborough

The A1(M), the A47 and the parkways are fast roads with few safe places to stop. Out in the Fens, roads are narrow with drains on both sides. We send a vetted operator who knows these roads, with a flatbed, a winch and skates, at a price you agree before they set off.

  • Breakdown recoveryWon’t start, warning lights, flat battery, snapped belt. Home, garage or our yard.
  • Accident recoveryCrash on the A1, A47 or in town. Car cleared and stored safely, insurer kept informed.
  • Car transportBought a car miles away? We collect and deliver anywhere in England, Wales or Scotland.
  • Auction collectionCopart, BCA and online auctions. We collect with your lot number and buyer ID.
  • Non-runner transportMOT failure, project car, SORN car or scrap. Moved on a flatbed with a winch.
  • Van recoveryTransits, Sprinters and Vivaros up to 3.5 tonnes. Recovered with the load.
