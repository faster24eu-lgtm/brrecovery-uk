What we move for the trade

  • Stock moves between sites and from auctions.
  • Customer deliveries and collections.
  • Cars to and from bodyshops, MOT stations and specialists.
  • Part-exchange collections, including non-runners.

How it works

  1. Tell us the job: pick-up, drop-off, the vehicle and when it needs to move.
  2. Fixed price per job, agreed before we book it in.
  3. Moved and delivered, with photos at pick-up and drop-off if you want them.

Regular work

If you move cars every week, talk to us about a regular arrangement. Auctions such as Copart at Wisbech are covered on the auction collection page.

Who we work with

Independent dealers and used car lots, franchised dealers needing an extra truck, bodyshops and accident repair centres, MOT stations and garages, and online car sellers delivering to customers. Moves are usually between Peterborough, Stamford, Huntingdon, Spalding and the surrounding towns, and to and from auctions such as Copart at Wisbech.

Condition reports

On request, the driver photographs the car at collection and delivery and notes any existing damage, so there’s a clear record for you and your customer. Tell us at booking if you need this.

Same-day moves

Need a car moved today? Call in the morning and we’ll tell you honestly whether a truck is free. For planned work, a day or two’s notice gives us the best chance of fitting it around other jobs.

FAQ

Straight answers

Do you send photos at collection and delivery?

Yes, on request. Ask when you book.

Do you collect from Copart Wisbech for dealers?

Yes, regularly. Send us the lot numbers and release details and we’ll collect and deliver.

Can you move more than one car at once?

Sometimes, depending on the cars and the trucks available. Tell us what you need and we’ll tell you the options.
