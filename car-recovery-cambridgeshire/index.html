Cambridgeshire splits into very different driving. In the north, Peterborough’s parkways and the A1(M) carry fast traffic. In the east, the fen roads around Wisbech, March and Chatteris are long and narrow with drains on both sides. In the south, the A14 and the A428 carry heavy freight into Cambridge. We cover all three.
